# The Guild wireless opportunity

Give the Guild a practical benefit on a bill households already pay. Angel could earn recurring contribution as members choose and keep the service. Validate household demand, provider and support costs, acquisition payback, and renewal compared with the existing Guild-only business. Evaluate launch and scale independently. Keep existing membership billing intact and treat any annual cash-timing effect separately from earned revenue or retention.

The consumer figures in CALLED-OFFER.md are illustrative. The earlier internal sensitivity case is superseded as pitch material. Angel and its partners would shape the operating structure, customer responsibilities and commercial terms after validation.
