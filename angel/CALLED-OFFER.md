# Called household offer: recommended negotiating target

November 14, 2026. Proposal, not an available service, signed quote or agreed split. Supersedes the withdrawn $90/free-Guild/$14-cost scenario. Latest user direction replaces film titles with original family communication photography while preserving Angel's depth, typography and material treatments.

## Consumer offer

Called Member: $23 per active line per month for a household with an eligible paid Guild membership. Proposed standard rate $28; $5 monthly member difference on every active line, no two-line cap. Rates target taxes and fees included. One line $23; two $46; three $69; four $92; five $115; six $138. Monthly member value versus proposed standard: $5/$10/$15/$20/$25/$30. These savings compare two proposed Called prices, not a customer's current carrier or a historical selling price.

Keep existing Guild membership billed separately. No free-membership replacement, credit wallet, extra votes, forced early annual renewal or loss of prepaid time. Annual and monthly paid members receive the same rate. Eligibility/account linking across billing channels and gifts needs Angel agreement. After paid membership ends, proposed standard rate begins at a subsequent billing cycle with advance notice and easy cancellation; no retroactive clawback of already earned discounts. Annual service remains owed through the paid-through date even if renewal is canceled.

Target service: unlimited domestic talk/text; 30 GB high-speed data per line; at least 1 Mbps after that; 5 GB hotspot within the high-speed allowance. Compatible unlocked BYOD; assisted number transfer; no activation fee; named care team; monthly wireless with no long-term commitment. Network, priority/congestion, roaming, exact coverage and all limits must be quoted and validated. Do not call this unlimited high-speed data or equivalent service to a competitor. No handset subsidies or phone-wide safety/filtering claims.

## Why a household would care

A rate comparable to low-cost alternatives, a material member benefit on each line, help switching, straightforward service and a brand centered on staying connected. Four lines earn $240/year in member value versus the proposed standard rate. That exceeds the current $159 annual Premium promotion, but is NOT a membership reimbursement, free Guild entitlement or guaranteed net savings versus another carrier. Guild stays separate. Family communication artwork shows calling home, sharing good news, checking in, hearing a familiar voice and laughing together.

## Operator economics and split

At four lines: net customer wireless receipts $92; target all-in operating burden $72 ($18 per active line); residual $20. Proposed residual allocation: Angel 70% ($14), operator 20% ($4), Freeman Signal 10% ($2). The operator also receives the contracted amounts included in the $72 to deliver service; do not compare its $4 residual with its total receipts.

$18 is a procurement ceiling/target, not a forecast of a carrier rate. An illustrative budget is network $14, platform $1, care $1, payments/fraud/refunds $1 and taxes/fees reserve $1. These are unverified negotiating allocations; actual costs may differ. Porting/eSIM, operator compensation and any excluded variable cost must fit within the aggregate $18. Fixed minimums and startup capital require a separate quote/ramp. No unsupported demand or low-tax assumption can close a gap.

Member benefit is already reflected in the net $23 price; do not subtract $5 again. Guild-only contribution baseline is unchanged for existing members, before any real retention change. No new membership proceeds, retention uplift or upfront annual cash is included in the wireless case. Any acquisition or new Guild contribution must be shown as a separate cohort after fulfillment/royalty/ticket/discount costs and attribution.

## Partnership proposal

Start as a managed branded service. Angel supplies brand approval, admitted Guild eligibility verification and agreed distribution; operator owns contracted network, activation, billing, care, refunds, tax administration and assigned compliance. Freeman Signal sources/structures the deal and coordinates delivery. Specify who funds losses, minimums, deposits, reserves and startup expenses. Allocate acquisition expenses before determining anyone's true return. Media inventory has opportunity cost.

Customer, permitted data, billing visibility, export/portability, service levels, exit/migration assistance and option-to-internalize terms belong in the initial agreement. A 70/20/10 split is a proposed starting point conditional on cost inclusions and responsibilities. No contract ownership, gross revenue presentation or enterprise-value uplift is established by that percentage.

Annual Guild: optional ordinary annual conversion for households choosing it; current promo eligibility applies. Do not ask existing annual households to prepay another year to access the rate. Test renewal/retention against Guild-only control. No early-recognition assumption or annual cash in wireless contribution.
