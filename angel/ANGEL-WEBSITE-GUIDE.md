## Typography

Actual family: **Angel Sans**, not Inter. The public stylesheet defines regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600 and bold 700 plus light/italic and condensed companions. These previews use the matching public weight-specific font URLs; no proprietary font binary is redistributed in the kit. Production font permissions are not established by a public URL.

Measured Guild/home campaign display at 1280px: 44px size, 46px line-height, weight 700, normal tracking, white. At 390px: 32px size, 34px line-height, weight 700, normal tracking. Measured secondary acquisition heading: 26px/28px, weight 600. Body foundation: 16px/24px, weight 400. CTA: 16px/24px, weight 600.

Runtime follows those measured primary sizes. Section display at 36px/38px is a documented proposed hierarchy variant for original wireless copy; phone sections use 26px/28px. No large 90-140px editorial headlines or exaggerated negative tracking remain. Product wordmark and social-card lettering are outlined from Angel Sans reference fonts with original product symbols. They are not copied Angel masterbrand marks.

## Controls and shapes

Measured Get Started control: 8px corner radius, 8px 24px padding, 56px minimum height, gold background, dark text, Angel Sans 600. Primary consumer actions now follow this geometry. Navigation and secondary actions are smaller variants with visible focus. New cards use an 8px radius as a proposed consistent product variant. Avoid pill buttons and ornamental shadows.

Use real buttons, links, native select/date controls, explicit labels, inline errors and deliberate focus transitions. Preserve natural document scrolling and reduced-motion preferences. The family calculator must update price, average per-line rate, the next-line increment and Guild eligibility at every line change.

## Composition and imagery

Follow Angel's confident, compact type, cinematic imagery, visible value proposition and clear acquisition path. The original Called family image is concept artwork, not a member photograph or an Angel endorsement. Guild Wireless uses a community composition; Good Word uses conversational forms. These are proposed product expressions inside the reconstructed typography/color/control system, not undocumented claims of official layouts.

## Voice and offer hierarchy

Lead with the household's concrete value: an illustrative four-line family price with one eligible Guild Premium membership included. Explain how every added line changes the bill. Speak plainly about family, stories and belonging. Humor can be observant, never spiritual pressure. No fabricated testimonials, launch scarcity or official approval. Do not describe a hypothetical price as contracted, claim cheapest service, promise a network/data allowance or imply phone-wide filtering.

The waitlist and annual journeys are interactive previews with fixed sample contact data. Included Guild membership, prepaid-term credit and optional advance annual purchase must never double-count the same benefit. One household gets one membership; extra lines do not create more votes.
