The offer,
in plain words.

The proposed $25 credit applies once per eligible household after a qualifying wireless activation, not when you join the interest list. Its funding and combination with any recurring member credit would need partner approval.

Annual membership is optional. You would receive real Guild benefits independently of whether Called launches. Your membership purchase would not guarantee a wireless price, coverage, launch date or invitation date.

An annual extension is a proposed new mechanism. If you pay through Apple, Google, Roku, Amazon or another platform, your existing subscription would need its supported billing path. Do not buy a second membership or cancel paid-through time to join this concept.
