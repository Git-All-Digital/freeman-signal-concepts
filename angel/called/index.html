Called / Answer what matters.

Good questions deserve clear answers

Before you
make the call.

Can I sign up for this service today?

This is a partnership concept prepared by Freeman Signal for discussion with Angel. It is not a launched service or an approved Angel offer. You can explore a free waitlist and annual Guild offer through the interactive preview. Sample details stay in the current page; no request or payment is sent.

What if I already paid for an annual Guild membership?

Four or more active lines would include one Guild Premium membership. If you already prepaid annually, the proposal gives an equivalent wireless credit while that paid term remains, then includes eligible Guild access. No duplicate membership or lost prepaid time.

What are the plan price and network?

They have not been set. Pricing, data allowances, taxes, coverage, device support and operating responsibilities require provider agreements before launch. The calculator shows an illustrative family price ladder. Final service allowances, network terms, taxes, fees and eligibility are not yet agreed.

Do more phone lines mean more votes?

No. Wireless line count would not add content influence. Angel's existing membership and family-profile voting rules would continue to govern participation.

Does this filter or protect everything on my phone?

No phone-wide filtering or family-safety capability is proposed here. Angel's content standards and parental tools concern its entertainment platform.

How would this help Angel?

The proposed business would earn recurring contribution from wireless service after its costs and member benefits. Angel could choose to reinvest that contribution in its business and mission. No particular film funding allocation is promised.
