Three original product identities, grounded in Angel's current visual world.

What was observed.

Angel's live website serves Angel Sans and uses charcoal, warm neutrals, copper, teal and mint. These previews use that visual vocabulary, with original product wordmarks, compositions and concept artwork.

A formal, complete Angel style guide has not been supplied or verified. Observed website tokens are evidence, not a claim of approved product branding. Production rights, font use, final marks and lockups require Angel's confirmation.

Angel Sans is the observed official typeface. These public previews use the openly licensed Inter as a review substitute, pending Angel's font authorization. The new wordmarks and original artwork are proposed assets, not Angel-approved marks.
