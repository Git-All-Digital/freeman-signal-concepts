Three original product identities, grounded in Angel's current visual world.

What was observed.

Angel's live website serves Angel Sans and uses charcoal, warm neutrals, copper, teal and mint. These previews use that visual vocabulary, with original product wordmarks, compositions and concept artwork.

At Chad's direction, we reconstructed a working brand and style guide from Angel's current marketing website: rendered desktop/mobile typography, source CSS, color tokens, controls and compositions. These pages are checked against that website-derived guide; it is not an Angel-issued manual or approval.

These previews now reference the same Angel Sans family and weight-specific public font assets used by Angel's current website. The working guide records exact sources, measured styles and the difference between observed rules and proposed product identities. The new wordmarks and original artwork are proposed assets, not Angel-approved marks.
