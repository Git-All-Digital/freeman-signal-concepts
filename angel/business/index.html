Three paths worth comparing

Choose the relationship.
Price the responsibilities.

Referral

Test whether a simpler referral fee or recurring commission produces better net value than a branded operation, after the media and trust it consumes.

Managed branded service

Angel leads brand and member experience. Named operating partners handle agreed activation, billing, care and compliance. Establish reporting, service levels, customer rights and a clear distribution waterfall.

Greater ownership

Compare in-house capabilities and regulatory responsibility with continued outsourcing. Contract assignment, customer portability, minimum commitments, financing and consent determine what is actually transferable. No subscriber threshold automatically creates enterprise value.

A launch that earns more investment

Prove the switch.
Prove the repeat value.

A free waitlist can qualify service area, unlocked phone, remaining device balance, lines and switching window. A marketing audience is not an activated customer base.

Test a clearly specified offer against a credible alternative. Track paid activation, successful number transfer, cost to serve, retained wireless contribution, Guild-only versus combined retention, ticket use and brand complaints.

Obtain quote-backed launch and scale cases. Show startup cash, peak funding, minimum commitments, incentives, vendor responsibility and Angel's actual income. Preserve member prepaid terms and a supported path for third-party billing.

Annual Guild membership

A useful option.
A separate decision.

Annual conversion can bring cash forward and may support retention. Angel's CFO describes those advantages in its current earnings call. It does not establish that a new wireless-linked annual extension would outperform the existing annual offer.

Distinguish new Guild acquisition, monthly-to-annual conversion and existing annual renewal cash brought forward. Deduct discounts, incentives, displaced renewals, refunds and any overlapping wireless benefits. No annual prepayment is counted twice.

Angel reports membership cash initially as deferred revenue, with recognition allocated across membership service, tickets and merchandise. Prepayment does not establish immediate recognition of an entire year.
